**Every Step Closer to Your First Yes.**

RoleClear is a career intelligence platform built for students and early-career applicants who are tired of applying blindly.

Instead of being just another job board or application tracker, RoleClear helps you understand **whether a job is worth applying to, how well your resume fits it, what you are missing, and what happens after you apply.**

🔗 **Live App:** https://role-clear.vercel.app  
⚙️ **API:** https://roleclear-api.onrender.com

---

## Why RoleClear?

Applying for internships and entry-level roles is often a guessing game.

You find a job, upload your resume, apply, and wait — without knowing:

- whether your resume actually matches the role
- which requirements you are missing
- whether the job posting looks trustworthy
- how much tailoring your resume needs
- where your application currently stands
- whether an employer email means interview, rejection, confirmation, or something else

RoleClear brings that entire workflow into one place.

### The workflow

**Discover → Verify → Match → Tailor → Apply → Track → Learn**

---

## What makes RoleClear different?

Most career platforms solve only one part of the application process.

RoleClear connects the complete journey.

### Resume Intelligence

Understand your fit before applying instead of relying only on keyword matching.

### Job Quality Signals

Analyze job listings for useful signals and potential warning signs.

### Application Intelligence

Track applications, interviews, offers, rejections, and recruiter activity in one place.

### Career Inbox

Connect Gmail and automatically turn employer emails into meaningful application signals.

### Explainable Scoring

Instead of showing only a score, RoleClear explains **why** your resume matches or does not match a role.

---

## Features

### Smart Apply

Paste a job description and get a structured analysis before applying.

- Resume Fit Score
- Requirements breakdown
- Skill gap detection
- Job quality signals
- Ghost-risk indicators
- Clear reasoning behind the score

### Resume Tailoring

Identify exactly what should be improved for a specific role without blindly rewriting your entire resume.

### Application Tracker

Manage your complete job search pipeline:

`Applied → Interview → Offer → Rejected`

### Career Inbox

Connect your Gmail account and let RoleClear detect career-related emails such as:

- application confirmations
- recruiter messages
- interview updates
- offers
- rejections

Relevant emails are classified and can be linked directly to your applications.

### Career Feed

Keep important job-search activity and updates in one place instead of switching between multiple platforms.

### Analytics

Understand your application journey through useful career metrics and application patterns.

---

## Built for Students and Freshers

RoleClear is designed specifically around the problems faced by people entering the industry:

- limited professional experience
- hundreds of similar job listings
- unclear eligibility requirements
- constant resume tailoring
- scattered recruiter communication
- difficulty tracking multiple applications

The goal is simple:

> **Apply with context, not guesswork.**
